# Business Development Intern (Sales + Marketing) – Remote

Company: Bitecodes
Location: Work from home
Duration: 3 months
Stipend: ₹2,000 – ₹5,000 /month + incentive on every client you bring in
Openings: 2
Start: Immediately

## About the internship

We are a small software company. We build websites, mobile apps and AI tools for clients, and we have a couple of our own products too. Right now most of our work comes through referrals. We want someone who can go out and find clients for us – in India and outside.

You will work directly with the founders. No layers, no fixed script. If you find a better way to reach people, we will try it.

What you will actually do:

- Find companies that need a website, app or AI tool built and figure out who to talk to there
- Reach out on LinkedIn, email and Upwork. Get them on a call with us
- Follow up. Most deals come from the 3rd or 4th message, not the 1st
- Post about our work on LinkedIn and Instagram a few times a week
- Keep a simple sheet of who you contacted and what happened

What we care about:

- Your English is good enough to email a client in the US or UK without us checking it
- You are not shy about messaging strangers
- You have some interest in tech. You do not need to code, but you should be able to explain what a web app is
- You show up daily and tell us what happened, good or bad

What you get:

- ₹2,000 to ₹5,000 a month depending on hours and experience
- A cut of every deal you bring in (we will agree the % before you start)
- Certificate and a recommendation letter
- Full-time offer if it works out
- Flexible hours, 4 hours a day minimum

## Skills
English Proficiency (Spoken), English Proficiency (Written), Lead Generation, Email Marketing, LinkedIn Marketing, MS-Excel

## Perks
Certificate, Letter of recommendation, Flexible work hours, Job offer
