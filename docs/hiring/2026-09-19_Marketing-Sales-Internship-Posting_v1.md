## About Bitecodes
Bitecodes builds software products and delivers web, mobile and AI development services to clients in India and abroad.

## Selected intern's day-to-day responsibilities
1. Research and build lists of target clients (startups, agencies, SMEs) in India, the US, UK, Australia and the Middle East.
2. Run outreach on LinkedIn, email and freelance marketplaces (Upwork, Fiverr, Clutch) and book discovery calls.
3. Qualify inbound leads and hand them to the founders for closing.
4. Write and schedule marketing content: LinkedIn posts, case studies, cold-email sequences.
5. Track every lead in the CRM and report weekly pipeline numbers.
