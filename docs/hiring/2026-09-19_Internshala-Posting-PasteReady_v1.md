# Internshala – Paste-Ready Posting

Internshala needs an employer account with a verified mobile number before posting. Once registered at internshala.com/employer, click Post an internship and paste the fields below. Internshala's form uses fixed fields, so the copy is split accordingly.

Internship profile: Business Development (Sales)
Skills required: English Proficiency (Spoken), English Proficiency (Written), Lead Generation, Email Marketing, LinkedIn Marketing, MS-Excel
Internship type: Work from home
Number of openings: 2
Start date: Immediately
Duration: 3 months
Stipend: Fixed, Rs 1,000 to 5,000 per month (Internshala will also ask about performance incentives; tick yes and describe as "commission on every closed deal, percentage agreed before joining")
Perks: Certificate, Letter of recommendation, Flexible work hours, Job offer

## About the internship (paste into the responsibilities box)

We are a small software company. We build websites, mobile apps and AI tools for clients, and we have a couple of our own products too. Right now most of our work comes through referrals. We want someone who can go out and find clients for us, in India and outside. You will work directly with the founders. No layers, no fixed script.

Selected intern's day-to-day responsibilities include:

1. Find companies that need a website, app or AI tool built and figure out who to talk to there
2. Reach out on LinkedIn, email and Upwork and get them on a call with us
3. Follow up. Most deals come from the 3rd or 4th message, not the 1st
4. Post about our work on LinkedIn and Instagram a few times a week
5. Keep a simple sheet of who you contacted and what happened

## Who can apply (paste into the "who can apply" box)

1. Your English is good enough to email a client in the US or UK without us checking it
2. You are not shy about messaging strangers
3. You have some interest in tech. You do not need to code, but you should be able to explain what a web app is
4. You can give at least 4 hours a day and report daily on what happened, good or bad

## About the company (paste into company profile if empty)

Bitecodes is a small software studio from Ahmedabad, India, working remote-first since 2021. We build websites, web and enterprise applications, SaaS products, APIs and AI automation for startups and businesses in India, the US, the UK, Australia and the Middle East. Website: www.bitecodes.com

## Assessment question to add (optional, recommended)

Pick any small business you know. In under 120 words, write the first message you would send to convince them they need a new website.
