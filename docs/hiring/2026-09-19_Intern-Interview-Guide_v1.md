# Business Development Intern – 30-minute Interview Guide

One call, 30 minutes, video on. Founder runs it. Take notes in the tracker CSV.

## 0–3 min: Set up
Tell them what Bitecodes does in two sentences and what the intern will actually do. Ask if they have questions before starting.

## 3–10 min: Their story
- Tell me about a time you had to convince someone who did not want to be convinced.
- Have you ever sold anything, raised money for an event, or messaged strangers for a reason? What happened?

Listen for: specifics, numbers, what they did when they heard no.

## 10–18 min: Live exercise
Share screen with one real prospect (a small clinic, a boutique, a coaching institute). Ask:
- Who at this company would you message and why?
- Read out the first three lines you would send.
- They reply "not interested right now". What do you write back?

Listen for: talks about the client's problem before talking about us, keeps it short, has a next step.

## 18–24 min: Working style
- How many hours a day can you give, and at what time of day?
- If you send 40 messages in a week and get zero replies, what do you do on Monday?
- What would you need from us to do this well?

## 24–28 min: Money, plainly
Explain the stipend range and how the commission works. Ask what they were expecting. Note any mismatch. Do not negotiate on the call.

## 28–30 min: Close
Tell them when they will hear back. Then decide the same day.

## Offer checklist
- Written offer by email: role, dates, hours, stipend, commission percentage and how it is calculated, when it is paid.
- One-page NDA covering client lists.
- Access on day one: company email or alias, LinkedIn page editor rights, shared tracker sheet.
- First-week target: 50 researched prospects, 25 messages sent, 1 call booked.
