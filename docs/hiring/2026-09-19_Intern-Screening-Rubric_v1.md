# Business Development Intern – Screening Rubric

Use this on every application before replying. Score each line 0, 1 or 2. Total out of 14.

| # | What to check | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Written English in the application itself | Errors that would embarrass us in front of a client | Readable, a few slips | Clean enough to send to a US/UK client as is |
| 2 | Did they read the posting? | Generic template, wrong role name | Mentions the role | References something specific (Upwork, follow-ups, the commission line) |
| 3 | Evidence of talking to strangers | None | Says they are comfortable with it | Shows it: college fest sponsorships, selling anything, cold DMs, part-time sales |
| 4 | Interest in tech | None | Vague | Can name what a web app or AI tool is and why a business would pay for one |
| 5 | Availability | Under 4 hours a day or unclear | 4 hours, some clash with college | 4+ hours daily, start within 2 weeks |
| 6 | LinkedIn or online presence | No profile or empty | Profile exists | Active: posts, decent headline, real network |
| 7 | Motivation for commission-based upside | Only asks about fixed stipend | Neutral | Asks how the commission works or what the deal sizes are |

Decision:
- 11 to 14: intro call within 48 hours.
- 7 to 10: send the 20-minute task (below) before deciding.
- Under 7: polite no.

## 20-minute task (send before the call for borderline candidates)

"Pick any two small businesses that you think need a new website or app. Write the first outreach message you would send to each. Keep each under 120 words. Send them back as plain text."

Grade on: is the message about the client's problem or about us, is there a clear next step, would you open it.

## Red flags that end the process

- Asks for the stipend to be paid in advance.
- Application copied from another candidate or obviously AI-written with no personal detail.
- Cannot do a video call.
