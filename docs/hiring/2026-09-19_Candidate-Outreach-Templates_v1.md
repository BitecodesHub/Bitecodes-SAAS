# Candidate Outreach Templates

For use on Wellfound "Find Talent", LinkedIn, and college placement cells. Nothing here has been sent. Replace the bracketed parts before use.

## 1. Wellfound or LinkedIn direct message to a candidate

Subject (Wellfound): Sales internship at a small software studio, remote

Hi [first name],

I run Bitecodes, a small software studio in Ahmedabad. We build websites, apps and AI tools for clients in India and abroad, and most of our work has come through referrals so far. I am looking for one or two interns to change that.

The job is finding companies that need something built and getting them on a call with us. LinkedIn, email, Upwork. Remote, 4 hours a day, 3 months. Stipend is small (Rs 1,000 to 5,000 a month) but you get a cut of every deal you bring in, and a full-time offer if it works.

Your profile stood out because [one specific thing: college fest sponsorship, a sales internship, active LinkedIn posts]. If this sounds interesting, reply here or apply at [job link] and I will set up a 30-minute call.

Ismail
Bitecodes

## 2. Follow-up after 4 days of silence

Hi [first name], quick follow-up on the sales internship at Bitecodes. No pressure if the timing is wrong. If you know someone in your batch who likes talking to people and wants sales experience, I would appreciate a forward.

## 3. Email to a college placement or training cell

Subject: Remote business development internship for [college] students

Bitecodes is a software studio based in Ahmedabad. We are offering two remote internships in business development and marketing for students in any stream, starting immediately.

Role: find and contact companies that need websites, apps or AI tools, and book discovery calls.
Duration: 3 months, minimum 4 hours a day, fully remote.
Stipend: Rs 1,000 to 5,000 per month plus commission on closed deals, certificate and recommendation letter, full-time offer for strong performers.
Apply: [job link] or email bitecodes.global@gmail.com with a CV and two lines on why.

We would be grateful if you could share this with final-year and pre-final-year students. I am happy to join a short call to answer questions.

Regards,
Ismail
Founder, Bitecodes
www.bitecodes.com

## 4. Reply to an applicant we are rejecting

Hi [first name], thank you for applying to Bitecodes. We are going ahead with other candidates for this round. I am keeping your details and will reach out if another role opens. Good luck with the search.

## 5. Reply to an applicant we want to interview

Hi [first name], thanks for applying. I would like to do a 30-minute video call. Please pick a slot here: [calendar link]. Before the call, send me two short outreach messages you would write to any two small businesses you think need a website. Under 120 words each, plain text is fine.

## Tone Check (Warm but Authoritative)

| Criterion | Score /5 | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Warmth | 4 | First-person, direct address, one personal hook per message. Rejection note thanks and leaves the door open. |
| Authority | 4 | States terms plainly (hours, stipend, commission) and sets a clear next step in every message. |
| Clarity | 5 | Short sentences, no jargon, one ask per message. |
| Brand voice consistency | 4 | Matches the plain, founder-led voice of the job posts. Avoids marketing adjectives. |
| Risk | 3 | The stipend is named honestly, which may reduce reply rate. The commission line carries the appeal, so keep it in every version. |

Overall: 4/5. Ready to send once bracketed fields are filled and the commission percentage is decided.
